Welcome

Set on a hill above the holy river Pampa, our parish has gathered the faithful of Vayalathala for worship since 1906. We belong to the Vayalathala District of the Nilackal Diocese of the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church.

Since 1906

Our History

From the first Holy Qurbana on the feast of St. Severus to the consecration of the church in 1991.

Our patron

St. Severus of Antioch

Patriarch of Antioch (AD 512–538), monk, theologian and teacher of the Church.
